Spend, sorted

Upload the statement file from your card issuer (Excel or CSV), or copy the purchases out of the monthly email and paste them below. The app finds the dates, merchants and amounts itself.
Added automatically once a month, on the day you set, under the card “Recurring”. Changes and deletions affect future months only; purchases already added stay.
Your data lives in this browser only. Press “Copy backup”, then paste the text into a note or email to yourself. To restore it on any device, paste it back here and press “Restore”, which replaces what’s currently saved.
