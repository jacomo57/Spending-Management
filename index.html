Rate and rules

Add an expense

Press “+ Add expense” above the list and fill in the row.

Changing a category

The app learns it: that merchant is sorted the same way next time.

Refunds

Enter a minus amount.

Split

Choose “Split…” in the category box to put part of a purchase in another category.

Tags

Write a #word in a note. Tag buttons appear above the list; press one to see only those.

Ignore and delete

Ignore keeps a row but leaves it out of the totals. ✕ deletes it, and Undo appears for a few seconds.

Finding things

The list shows the latest 5. Press Show all, or use the month, card and Search boxes.

Add income

Add a row, then choose “Income” in its category box. It counts as money in, not spending.

Paybacks

Choose a category on an income row (“Back: …”) and it lowers that category, like a partner paying you back.

Salary

Add it every month in Recurring, or import a payslip in Import.

On the main screen

The 4 biggest categories, or the ones you pick with ☆.

Editing

“All categories · Edit” lets you rename, change the icon, drag to reorder, ☆, delete, add, or go back to the 4 biggest.

Filtering

Press a category to see only its expenses.

How a card counts

In full, half (a joint card), a custom percentage, or not counted.

Default card

The ★ sets the card used for phone purchases that name no card.

Renaming

Renaming a card changes it everywhere.

Budgets

Set a monthly budget per category and a total. Leave a box empty for none.

On the main screen

Pick one month and All cards. Each bar fills toward its budget: amber from 80%, red when over.

Month starts on day

1 is the calendar month. Use your payday or card billing day.

Recurring items

Press “+ Add recurring”. A bill or income is added every month on the day you set.

When they appear

When the app opens or returns to the front, starting the month you add them.

Deleting one

Delete a generated row and it is not added again.

Statements

Type the card name, upload an Excel or CSV file or paste the rows, and press Import and sort. Rows you already have are skipped.

Foreign statements

Pick the currency next to the card rule. Each row is converted at its date's rate.

Payslips

Upload a PDF or picture; the net pay is added as income. Check the amount.

Phone purchases

They merge with their statement row when the amount and date match.

Home currency

In Account: what everything is shown in. Changing it offers to convert your saved amounts (you can undo).

Buying in

In Account: the currency new purchases are entered in. Set it to euro for a trip.

Paid and rate

A foreign purchase shows what you paid and the rate (home currency per 1 unit). Change the currency box if it was entered wrong.

Where rates come from

The European Central Bank, for the purchase date. Offline, the last known rate is used, or type your own.

Matching your card

Edit the amount at the top of the row to your card's real charge; the rate adjusts.

From the phone

A plain amount uses “Buying in”. A notification that names a currency (€50) is saved in it.

The report

More → Report shows Spent, Income and Left over, then breakdowns by category, card, tag and merchant.

Compare and trend

Compare puts two months side by side; the trend shows months over time.

Learned merchants

Change a category here to re-sort every purchase from that merchant.

Phone

A MacroDroid macro (Android) or a Shortcuts shortcut (iPhone), or a Wallet payment, adds purchases without opening the app. They are marked until a statement row confirms them.

Theme

Auto, Light or Dark, in the More menu. Remembered per device.
